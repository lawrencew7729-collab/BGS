About BGS Electrical Trading

Established to serve the growing infrastructure needs, we are a full-service electrical and energy company specializing in electrical supply, solutions, and contracting work for commercial, retail, and industrial clients.

From large blue-chip companies to small and medium enterprises, we stock all your electrical supply needs under one roof. This saves you the hassle of phoning different suppliers. If your requirements cannot be readily met from our huge stocks, we will immediately source specific custom products as needed.

Our team is composed of technically skilled and highly dedicated personnel working cohesively to promote value-added solutions with competitive pricing and a structure to suit your specific needs. We recognize that the key to running a successful business is understanding our clients' needs and exceeding their expectations through robust, quality project delivery.
